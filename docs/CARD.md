# present-together

Give better talks: an AI coaches you and builds the interactive parts.

## What it does
It coaches you on your message and on activities that let your audience take part, such as a phone poll where everyone commits to an answer first. The agent then builds, tests and, if you want, hosts the interactive parts, and you keep a method card for your next talk. You decide the message, the activity and your verdict on the first version of the activity.

## Interfaces
- Skill: `skills/interactive-classroom-presentation/SKILL.md`, with `AGENTS.md` as the entry for agents.

## Works best with
Any agent app that can open files and run programs builds and tests the activities; coaching alone works in almost any AI chat. Hosting a live activity is optional and can cost money; the agent shows the cost and asks first.

## Who it suits
Teachers and students preparing a talk, lecture or seminar that is not graded or submitted.

## Status
ready

## Install
- Claude Code: copy `skills/interactive-classroom-presentation` into `.claude/skills/` in your project folder, or paste the starter prompt from the README.
- Codex: copy `skills/interactive-classroom-presentation` into `.agents/skills/` in your project folder, or paste the starter prompt from the README.
- Other apps: paste the starter prompt from the README; the agent reads `AGENTS.md` and the skill from the repository.

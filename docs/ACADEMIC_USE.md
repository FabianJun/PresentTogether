# Academic use

**Use PresentTogether for talks that are not graded and not submitted to your institution, with your own material or material you may share.** Institution-provided teaching material stays out of this workflow; for that, use your institution's own process and approved AI tools.

Before reading any teaching file, the agent runs [a short permitted-use check](../skills/interactive-classroom-presentation/references/academic-use.md). It asks only what it does not already know: what the talk is for, where the material comes from and whether your institution restricts the AI tools you may use. The check sets this project's boundary; it is not institutional approval, does not certify compliance and does not protect you from misconduct findings.

## Why "ungraded" is not always enough

Many institutions restrict which AI tools may be used for anything submitted, including ungraded work, and some require you to declare AI use or keep your prompts. Some courses require parts of a student's work to be entirely their own. A paid AI subscription or privacy setting does not make a tool approved.

So:

- Use this workflow for practice, independent teaching and ungraded talks outside any submission. Graded work and anything you submit stay outside it.
- Share only your own material or material you may share. Lecture slides you received from your institution or others, course-platform material, assignment files, private case studies and other material your institution provided stay out. Your own slides are fine.
- Keep your reasoning, source checking and teaching decisions your own. Understand and test what the agent builds.
- When unsure, check your institution's current AI guidance, your module instructions or ask your lecturer.

This is the **conservative boundary chosen for this project**. It is narrower than many institutions' rules and does not claim that any institution bans all AI in assessed work.

## Institution profiles

Rules differ between institutions. Where someone has checked an institution's rules, the skill keeps a short profile in [the institutions folder](../skills/interactive-classroom-presentation/references/institutions/). The first profile is for [the institution](../skills/interactive-classroom-presentation/references/institutions/TEMPLATE.md), where this project began. To add yours, copy [the template](../skills/interactive-classroom-presentation/references/institutions/TEMPLATE.md). Original institutional documents and local paths are never included in this repository.

The software licence grants software permissions only. It does not grant academic permission, rights to institutional content or permission to bypass assessment rules.

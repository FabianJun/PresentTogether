# Academic-use boundary

**Use this workflow only for presentations that are not graded, with your own material that you are allowed to share. Do not upload or process institutional teaching materials using these external coding agents.**


This repository shares a technical example and a way to iterate on an independent learning activity. It is not a way around assessment rules. Its provider-neutral model recommendations do **not** make those providers approved tools for graded submissions.

## Why “ungraded” is not enough by itself

The locally supplied *AI learning strategy for students*, sections 6–7 and Appendix 4, restricts the tools used for submissions and sets disclosure, evidence-retention and student-only analysis requirements. The *programme handbook*, Appendix 1, also addresses tool restrictions and assessment consequences. Those documents were reviewed for this notice; they are not distributed in this repository.

In the reviewed strategy, Alexandria is the permitted submission tool, with institution-authenticated Microsoft Copilot as a specified fallback when Alexandria is unavailable. A paid external coding-agent subscription is not equivalent to that approval. The restrictions refer to submissions, not only to work receiving a grade.

Therefore:

- Do not use this external-agent workflow to create or revise a graded assessment or a graded submission.
- Do not supply institutional lecture slides, Moodle materials, assignment files, private case studies or other institution-provided content to it.
- Keep your reasoning, source checking and teaching decisions your own. Understand and test what the agent builds.
- Check the current institution's GenAI guidance, module instructions and approved tools with your lecturer or AI tutor. If anything is unclear, resolve it before using an external agent.

The no-graded-work and no-institution-material rules above are the **conservative use boundary chosen for this project**. They are not a quotation claiming that the institution bans all AI in all assessed work. The reviewed policy permits certain uses under specific conditions, and it may be updated.

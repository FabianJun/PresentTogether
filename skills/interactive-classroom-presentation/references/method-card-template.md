# Personal method card

At the end, help the person fill this in, ideally in their own words. Save it as `my-methods.md` in their project folder. If they already have one from an earlier talk, add to it instead of starting a new file.

Keep it to what they actually used and would use again. Do not fill it with methods they only heard about.

```markdown
# My methods for explaining and presenting

## [Talk title], [date]

### [Method name]
- What I did: [one sentence]
- Why it works: [one sentence, in my words]
- Next time I would: [one change]
- It does not fit when: [one situation]

### [Method name]
...

## What I want to try next
- [one method or idea]
```

Short reflection questions to ask first, one at a time:

1. What worked better than you expected?
2. What surprised you about the audience, or about your own talk?
3. What would you do differently next time?

# Rehearsal checks

Use the relevant checks for the activity. Record evidence and remaining limits, rather than merely marking every box complete.

## Learning and language

- Can a new participant explain their role, goal and choice after reading the first screen?
- Does each decision reveal a trade-off connected to the material?
- Are assumptions and illustrative numbers distinguishable from evidence?
- Can the teacher explain who benefits, who pays and who is exposed to risk?
- Does the debrief respond to the actual class result?
- Are notes natural to say aloud, with time for transitions and pauses?

## Rules and reliability

- Calculate every outcome for one round by hand and compare with the implementation.
- Check complete paths or appropriate invariants, including boundaries and cumulative totals.
- Try one person per group, a larger group, a tie, missing votes, and a completely empty group.
- Confirm that tie-breaking cannot override a clear majority.
- Submit twice, submit late, refresh a participant, and repeat a facilitator action.
- Disconnect and reconnect; verify both the saved choice and current phase.
- Check that private control links cannot be replaced by public room codes.
- Check that reset requires deliberate confirmation and affects only the intended room.

## Experience and delivery

- Test actual narrow phone layouts; keyboard and focus order; visible text contrast.
- Show only the projection, with browser chrome hidden using fullscreen.
- Scan the real room QR code from the back of the room when possible.
- Skip, reopen and finish the walkthrough; confirm that game state is unchanged.
- Follow all round notes and review a previous result; verify that explanations match.
- Open each speaker's private link and PDF; check slides, notes and handovers.
- Check the complete PDF and PowerPoint, including references and static example labels.
- Practise the offline fallback and explain how to back up and stop hosting.

Automated checks support this rehearsal. They do not establish classroom usability, accessibility conformance, or a production capacity guarantee by themselves.

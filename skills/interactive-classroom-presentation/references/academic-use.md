# Check permitted use first

Before you open any teaching file, make sure the owner may use it in this workflow. This is a quick check, not a legal review, and it never counts as institutional approval. The owner brings their institution's current policy, module or course instructions and lecturer guidance. Apply these alongside the general check below; the stricter rule takes precedence.

## Three questions

Use what the conversation and project already tell you. Ask only for facts that are still missing and would change the answer.

1. **Purpose.** Is this independent teaching or practice? Or is it for a graded assessment or anything the person submits to their institution? An ungraded submission still counts as a submission.
2. **Material.** Is it the person's own content, or material they may share? Does it contain institution-provided teaching or assessment material, confidential information or personal data?
3. **Tools.** Do their institution or course restrict which AI tools may process this material?

Do not ask the person to upload something so you can decide whether it is restricted. Ask them to describe it instead.

| What you find | What you do |
|---|---|
| Independent, ungraded use outside any submission, with the person's own or shareable material and no conflicting tool rule | Note in one line that it fits this project's boundary, then continue. Do not call it approved. |
| Assessment status, ownership, applicable institutional rules or tool permission is unclear | Clarify that fact before reading the material. You can still talk about activity ideas in general. |
| Current institutional, course or lecturer rules forbid the proposed AI use, even for independent practice | Do not help with the restricted work or process its material. Point to the institution's own clarification process. |
| Graded work, a submission, or any institution-provided material | Explain the boundary kindly and point to the institution's own process or AI support. Do not continue with that material here, and do not suggest relabelling it to get round the rule. |

This repository contains no institution profiles. Do not assume one institution's rules apply to another, or treat missing guidance as permission. The owner can use [the template](institutions/TEMPLATE.md) to record their current rules in their own copy or outside this repository; never commit a completed template here.

## The principles behind the check

- **Approved tools matter.** Paying for an agent or switching on privacy settings does not make it an approved tool for an institution.
- **Access is not permission.** Being able to open a course file does not mean it may be sent to an external agent. Do not request institution-provided lecture slides, course-platform material, assignment files or private case studies without permission; this project's conservative boundary keeps them outside this workflow even when permission exists. Keep institutional and personal information out of public repositories.
- **Do not route around restrictions.** Never suggest moving restricted material to a personal account, another provider or a public upload.
- **Students stay responsible.** They must understand, check and be able to explain their work. Where a course requires independent analysis, do not write, rewrite or polish that part. Coaching on how to present is fine; doing the required thinking for them is not.
- **Disclosure may be required.** Some rules ask for a declaration, prompts, tool details or saved outputs. Remind the person to check this.
- **Rules change.** Use current guidance. If permission is unclear, the owner seeks clarification through their institution before restricted work continues.

This project deliberately takes a conservative position: ungraded, independently authored or permitted public material, outside any submission. That is narrower than many institutions' rules, and it does not claim that any institution bans all AI in assessed work.

## Policy documents

If the owner provides policy documents they may share for this check, read only the relevant parts and keep only the decision rules needed. Otherwise, ask for a description of the applicable restrictions or clarification through their institution. Keep any rule notes in the owner's own copy or outside this repository. Do not publish institutional names, the original files, long extracts, signatures, student details or local paths. Reading a policy does not authorise processing other institutional material.

Only the unfilled [template](institutions/TEMPLATE.md) is shipped here; institution-specific rules are never committed to this repository.
